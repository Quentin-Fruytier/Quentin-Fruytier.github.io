Sculpting the Latent Space

Disentanglement with WAE-MMD and Programmable Priors

The goal

Disentanglement asks for two different things

1

Mutually independent latents

No latent unit can be predicted from the others, over the whole dataset.

2

Semantically meaningful latents

Each unit lines up with a real factor: shape, scale, position, colour.

Without supervision, (2) is not identifiable: many independent representations fit the same data. Unsupervised methods can only aim at (1). We measure (1), enforce it, and then inject (2) through the prior.

Hyvärinen & Pajunen 1999; Locatello et al. 2019, 2020; Khemakhem et al. 2020

5

Why the score is safe to trust

LPS can only underestimate entanglement

R² of any regressor ≤ η², the share of a latent’s variance the others truly explain

No regressor beats the conditional mean. So an LPS of 0.5 proves that at least half of the latent information is entangled, whatever the ground-truth factors are: a rejection test that needs no labels.

The fix · SWAE-MMD

Match the aggregate posterior to prior samples

maximise E[ log q(x) ] − λ · MMD²( q(z), p(z) )

reconstruct the input, and make the distribution of all codes match the prior, using only samples of the prior

The regularizer acts on the batch, not on each sample: it sees the whole aggregate shape.

It needs no density for p(z), only a way to sample it. That is what makes the prior programmable.

A Wasserstein autoencoder variant (Tolstikhin et al. 2018) used for representation engineering · batch 512, λ = 0.3

Results · CIFAR-10

Independence holds as the latent space grows

Flat in the latent size

From 32 to 96 dimensions the MMD model stays low, at about 0.1 or below, while most baselines grow more entangled.

Insensitive to its knobs

Batch 64 to 2048, λ from 0.1 to 5, 1 to 5 kernel bandwidths: only small changes in LPS and reconstruction.

Programmable prior 1 · semantic alignment

A few pseudo-labels lock latents to real factors

The prior becomes a joint over (z, f(u)) whose first k latents equal the pseudo-labels; MMD on the joint ties those latents to the labels and keeps the rest independent.
